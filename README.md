# Rimworld Races - Elves
![](About/Preview.png?raw=true) 
<p>Elves is the second module of the Rimworld Races series, heavily inspired in High Fantasy settings like Lord of the Rings and D&D.</p>
<p><h3>Personal Philosophy:</h3>As a former D&D player, I was always drawn to fill Rimworld with fantasy races. When the Lord of the Rims series by Jecrell was abandoned, I took the matter in my own hands, adapting the mod to my needs. Soon the fixing and tweaking wasn't enough and I start adding new content.</p><p>A second breakthrough came years later with the Biotech DLC and the Stoneborn mod by DetVisor. For compatibility sake, I converted all my D&D races into xenotypes, dwarves also, although I treat them as separate races. The Stoneborn mod gave me the possibility to add a dwarven style to truly complete the culture under of the mountain.</p>
<p>Like every mod I made, the Dwarves module is designed to be complemented by others to recreate the sensation of a living world. For example, if Rimworld Cooking Redux is loaded, the player will be able to cook porridge and make camomile infusion, among other things. Especially recommended alongside Aquiles Alchemy, because it will allow your dwarven colonists and traders to make good use of all the new ores and metals.</p>
<p><b>Recommended mod:</b> Aquiles Alchemy by Aquiles.</p>
